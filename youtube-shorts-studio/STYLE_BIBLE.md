# STYLE BIBLE

## Target Look
"Polished modern 3D children's animation" — think quality preschool TV, but ORIGINAL designs.

## Visual Requirements
- High-quality 3D rendering (soft subsurface, global illumination).
- Appealing stylized characters with smooth rounded shapes.
- Soft cinematic lighting with gentle rim light.
- Clean, semi-glossy materials (foam/plush for characters; rounded wood/grass for world).
- Detailed but readable environments — no visual clutter.
- Expressive oversized eyes and appealing expressions.
- Strong foreground/background separation (depth of field when useful).
- Saturated but pleasant colors.

## Camera & Composition
- Simple, readable compositions centered on action.
- Gentle pushes/pans; static backgrounds during character movement.
- Depth of field to isolate the subject.
- Camera should rarely be confusing; steady for Shorts.

## Avoid (hard rules)
- Creepy/uncanny faces, distorted hands/extra fingers, inconsistent eyes.
- Deformed bodies, inconsistent clothing, random objects.
- Any text/logo/watermark artifacts.
- Photorealistic humans.
- Overly complicated/confusing backgrounds.

## Motion Principles
- Simple controlled movement over chaotic movement.
- Only 1-2 actions per video clip.
- Natural body weight; squash & stretch feel.
- Background stable → foreground emotional.

## Recurring Environments (build as reusable sets)
1. **Cozy Den / Playroom** — Pip's home (warm, rounded, toys).
2. **Sunny Meadow** — soft green rolling hills, big friendly sun.
3. **Pebble Brook** — small stream, stepping stones, butterflies.
4. **Wonder Grove** — oversized friendly trees and giant flowers (scale-play).
5. **Candy Kitchen** — bright bakery for food-based stories.

## Audio Direction
Original or royalty-free only. Cheerful simple melody, memorable sound effects, positive tone. No copyrighted audio.
