# IDEAS — Pilot Batch (01)

Batch: PILOT-01

Cast: Pip (main, yellow bear), Nana (mint rabbit), Flippy (coral fox), Momo (cream chick)

## CONCEPT IDEAS (10)

### C1 — "Pip's Bouncy Ball"
- **Premise:** Pip's ball bounces away into Wonder Grove and won't come back; Pip & friends chase it through a giant garden of oversized flowers, then realize the ball was rolling home all along.
- **Hook:** ball bonks a giant flower and keeps bouncing.
- **Emotion:** joy → mild frustration → relief/joy.
- **Characters:** Pip (+ cameo Momo).
- **Environment:** Wonder Grove (scale play).
- **Conflict:** ball escapes + a giant wheelbarrow-blocked path.
- **Escalation:** friends join (Nana & Flippy) each offer a funny failed method (Nana cups it, Flippy pounces).
- **Payoff:** gentle wind + slope rolls ball back to Pip; it lands at his feet.
- **Replay:** ball lands at start again → loops to "go!"

### C2 — "The Tiny Helpers"
- **Premise:** Nana drops all her acorn-collecting basket, tiny seeds scatter; Pip, Flippy, and Momo each help a different way, showing kindness.
- **Hook:** basket flips, hundreds of colorful seeds bounce.
- **Emotion:** warm/kindness.
- **Environment:** Sunny Meadow.
- **Conflict:** basket spill + wind picks up seeds.
- **Payoff:** all seeds collected, Nana's beaming; a friendly sun cheers.
- **Replay:** seed-scatter loop.

### C3 — "A Very Sticky Cake"
- **Premise:** In Candy Kitchen, Flippy tries to help bake but pours so. much. honey that the cake gets silly-sticky and sticky-funny things happen; they still make it delicious.
- **Hook:** giant honey drip from the jar.
- **Emotion:** comedy (cause-effect).
- **Environment:** Candy Kitchen.
- **Conflict:** over-sticky batter + Flippy's paws stuck.
- **Payoff:** they add "one more tiny sprinkle" and the cake pops out perfect → everyone grins.
- **Replay:** sweet-tease end.

### C4 — "Where's Momo?"
- **Premise:** Momo the chick hides (miniature) and the trio plays hide & seek; a tender "found you!" reunion. Looping peek-a-boo.
- **Hook:** Momo ducks behind a giant leaf, only the chirp gives her away.
- **Emotion:** sweet/care.
- **Environment:** Pebble Brook / under-bush sets.
- **Conflict:** they can't find her for a moment → gentle worry.
- **Payoff:** Momo pops out, everyone cuddles.
- **Replay:** peek-a-boo is endlessly replayable.

### C5 — "The Big Bubble"
- **Premise:** Pip blows one HUGE shimmering bubble that lifts and carries a tiny leaf up; Flippy chases it; the bubble pops on a flower and rains glitter-rain.
- **Hook:** the biggest bubble inflates.
- **Emotion:** wonder/beauty.
- **Environment:** Sunny Meadow.
- **Conflict:** bubble floats toward a spiky bush (could pop early).
- **Payoff:** it lifts gently and pops into a glitter-rain shower.
- **Replay:** bubble-rise loop.

### C6 — "A Snack For The Sun"
- **Premise:** The friendly sun looks droopy/cloudy; the friends gather a "sun snack" of shiny things and the sun brightens and smiles. Kindness/beauty.
- **Hook:** sun frowns (unusual!).
- **Emotion:** empathy/kindness.
- **Environment:** Wonder Grove golden hour.
- **Conflict:** sun not smiling.
- **Payoff:** sun beams, world glows warmer, friends wave.
- **Replay:** sunrise-loop.

### C7 — "Splash! Pebble Bridge"
- **Premise:** At Pebble Brook the stepping stones wobble; Pip bravely tests them, Flippy slips with a silly PLOP, and they build a tiny safe bridge together. Teamwork.
- **Hook:** a stone wobbles with a big wobble-sfx.
- **Emotion:** brave/funny teamwork.
- **Environment:** Pebble Brook.
- **Conflict:** Flippy's splash.
- **Payoff:** a wood-plank bridge; everyone crosses together.
- **Replay:** crossing-loop.

### C8 — "The Mystery Box"
- **Premise:** A small gift box arrives (curiosity); they guess in silly ways (Nana thinks it's Nana-shaped, Flippy thinks it's an acorn) → it's a wind-up that hops! Curiosity/discovery.
- **Hook:** a *knock knock* + wiggling box.
- **Emotion:** curiosity/delight.
- **Environment:** Cozy Den.
- **Conflict:** can't open it (too tight) → they learn to ask a grown-up tree friend.
- **Payoff:** the hopping toy winds up and does a little dance.
- **Replay:** box-wiggle ↔ hop loop.
- **Series-ready:** "Mystery Box" object-of-the-week.

### C9 — "A Too-Tall Tower"
- **Premise:** The friends try to build a block tower as tall as a sunflower; each funny failure (Flippy sneezes, Momo waddles too close) → they cooperate, stacking gently, winning the "all-together" height.
- **Hook:** wobble near the top.
- **Emotion:** playful teamwork.
- **Environment:** Cozy Den.
- **Conflict:** topple w/ funny cause.
- **Payoff:** stable tower + a little flag on top; sun cheers.
- **Replay:** rebuild-loop.

### C10 — "A Song That Pops"
- **Premise:** Pip starts a silly original melody using found sounds (drip, clap, boing); friends join with their own "instruments," building a tiny original music moment. Music/rhythm.
- **Hook:** a single "boing!"
- **Emotion:** joy/music.
- **Environment:** Sunny Meadow.
- **Conflict:** none heavy; one funny dropped beat (Flippy trip).
- **Payoff:** they finish together with a cheer.
- **Replay:** loopable beat; strong for music-driven kids.
- **Audio:** requires original looping melody (flagged).

## SELECTED CONCEPT
**C4 — "Where's Momo?"** (Total 62). Rationale:
- Highest emotional + replay combination. Peek-a-boo is a near-universal toddler behavior → massive retention & loop replay.
- Minimal/zero dialogue → fully language-light, global-friendly.
- Simple, gentle conflict → perfect for a *pilot* that establishes the cast and warmth without complexity.
- Very high OpenArt feasibility (simple environment, one hiding action, tender payoff).
- Great first impression: establishes all 4 characters in one warm, short story.
