# SCRIPT — EP-001 "Where's Momo?"

**Episode ID:** EP-001
**Concept ref:** PILOT-01 / C4
**Title option A:** "Where's Momo? 🐣 (Peek-a-Boo!)"
**One-line premise:** Momo the chick hides, the trio searches, and a tender "found you!" peek-a-boo payoff loops forever.
**Duration target:** ~28–35s (Shorts sweet spot)
**Language:** Language-light; only gentle, non-verbal vocal sounds ("boing", "peep", giggle). No spoken dialogue needed.
**Characters:** Pip, Nana, Flippy, Momo.
**Environment:** Pebble Brook — soft green bank, giant friendly leaves, a tiny wooden bridge, warm sunlight, small stream.

## Scene Flow (visual beats)

- **S1 — HOOK (0–2s):** Momo's fluffy peep chirps; she duck behind one big leaf in the warm meadow. Wide eyes everywhere. Loud cheerful music sting starts.
- **S2 — SETUP (2–8s):** Pip, Nana, Flippy turn to where she was. Pip points. Nana smiles warmly. Flippy tiptoes toward the leaf.
- **S3 — ESCALATION (8–22s):** Flippy peeks behind leaf → empty (Momo already moved). Flippy checks a log → empty. Nana checks under a flower → empty. Pip taps a hollow tree → empty. Growing silly "where?!" energy with comedic sound effects (boing, flutter). Momo giggles somewhere offscreen, giving a tiny "peep!" signal.
- **S4 — PAYOFF (22–28s):** A leaf trembles + tiny "peep". All three freeze, turn slowly, eyes wide. They pull the leaf aside together → MOMO! Big shared gasp then warm hug/cuddle pile. Momo giggles joyfully.
- **S5 — ENDING/LOOP (28–33s):** Momo peeks out of the group pile and gives a tiny wave and "peep!". The group dissolves into giggles. Final frame freezes on Momo winking → visually hints it can loop (she'll hide again next time).

## Music / SFX
- Cheerful original/royalty-free ukulele+clap loop, warm key (C major, ~120bpm).
- SFX: soft "boing", gentle "flutter", bright "peep", giggle burst, a warm cymbal "ding" on the reveal.
- **All audio must be original or royalty-free — no copyrighted tracks.**

## Emotion
Warm, curious, playful, tender. No tension that scares.

## Replay
The "hide → found → peek → hide again" emotional loop is inherently replayable.
