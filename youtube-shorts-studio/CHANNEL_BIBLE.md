# CHANNEL BIBLE

## Channel Identity

**Working title:** "Tiny Pop" (final name pending — see name options)
**Concept:** 3D-animated children's Shorts for ages 3–7. Original, colorful, character-driven mini-adventures with strong replay value.

### Core Mission
Produce scalable, ORIGINAL, high-quality 3D children's Shorts using OpenArt MCP as the primary visual/video generation engine. Every Short must deliver a tiny, complete, satisfying story that a child understands without needing language.

### North-Star Principles
1. **Originality** — never reuse copyrighted characters, songs, scripts, or branding.
2. **Clarity** — one simple conflict, one satisfying payoff, visually understood.
3. **Consistency** — recurring characters keep their identity across every episode.
4. **Polish** — modern 3D-animation quality bar (colorful, soft cinematic lighting, appealing faces).
5. **Retention** — a hook in the first 2 seconds, frequent visual changes, replayable loops.

## Brand Fundamentals

- **Logo concept:** A cute round character (see CHARACTER_BIBLE) peeking over a soft circle/yolk, with friendly lowercase wordmark. Bright gradient background.
- **Mascot:** Pip (main character) — see CHARACTER_BIBLE.
- **Color palette:**
  - Primary: Sunny Yellow `#FFD34E`
  - Secondary: Sky Blue `#4FC3F7`
  - Accent: Coral `#FF7A6B`
  - Earth: Fresh Green `#7ED957`
  - Neutral: Warm Cream `#FFF6E5`
- **Typography:** Rounded, friendly, high-readability sans (e.g. Baloo 2 / Fredoka style). Avoid thin or decorative fonts on screen text.
- **Thumbnail style:** One dominant character, huge readable emotion, saturated background, minimal-to-no text, strong contrast, readable at phone size. Never deceptive.
- **Visual language:** soft rounded shapes, global illumination, clean materials, expressive oversized eyes, gentle camera moves.

## Recurring Character System
See CHARACTER_BIBLE.md. Three named characters + one animal sidekick form the core cast. Supporting one-off characters introduced only when a story needs them.

---

## Monetization Safety
- All content original: characters, scripts, music, thumbnails.
- No copyrighted songs, assets, or characters.
- No deceptive metadata or thumbnails.
- No mass-produced near-identical videos; each Short has real creative differences.
- **Note:** Monetization eligibility is decided by YouTube's current policies. We build to maximize eligibility but never guarantee it.
