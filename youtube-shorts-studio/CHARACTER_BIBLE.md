# CHARACTER BIBLE

The rule: **consistency wins.** A child recognizes the channel by the characters. Never redesign a character between scenes unless the story *requires* it.

---

## MAIN CHARACTER — "PIP"

**Type:** Round, cheerful 3D blob-bear cub (original design; NOT a licensed character).
**Role:** Lead; carries the story, the heart, and the emotion in every episode.

### Visual Lock
- **Body:** Small rounded pear-shaped torso, soft foam-like material, smooth.
- **Color:** Sunny yellow `#FFD34E` body, warm cream belly patch.
- **Face:** Two large glossy dark-brown eyes with catchlight, tiny round nose, small smiling mouth. No other facial features beyond these for a clean child-friendly silhouette.
- **Ears:** Two small round ears on top (bear-like).
- **Arms/Legs:** Short stubby rounded limbs; hands are simple rounded mitts (never 5 fingers — avoids distortion).
- **Accessories:** A single teal scarf always worn; signature.
- **Proportions:** ~2-3 head-length chibi style.
- **Signature expressions:** Wide-eye surprise (hook), open-mouth giggle (joy), puffed-cheek determination (problem), relieved soft smile (payoff).
- **Signature pose:** Peeking over a surface / waving with one mitt.

### Personality
Curious, kind, determined, easily surprised, giggly. Age-appearance: toddler/preschool. Never mean.

### OpenArt Generation Strategy
Use a consistent locked base prompt (below) for EVERY gift of this character, plus scene-specific action suffixes. Prefer OpenArt character/reference tools to keep identity.

## SUPPORTING CHARACTER 1 — "NANA"

**Type:** Small round mint-green rabbit-like friend (original).
**Role:** Best friend; warm, gentle, worried-but-kind. Contrast to Pip's energy.
- **Color:** Mint green `#7ED9C4` body, white inner ears and muzzle.
- **Face:** long soft ears that flop when happy, big friendly eyes, two front buck teeth.
- **Accessories:** pink polka-dot bow on one ear.
- **Personality:** gentle, cautious, supportive, gives the emotional beat.

## SUPPORTING CHARACTER 2 — "FLIPPY"

**Type:** Tiny round coral fox-like friend (original).
**Role:** Fast, mischievous, funny; provides visual comedy and snappy energy.
- **Color:** Coral `#FF7A6B` body, cream chest, big pointy-but-cute ears.
- **Face:** cheeky grin, upturned eyes, bushy tail.
- **Accessories:** a single band-aid on one ear (fun signature, relatable to kids).
- **Personality:** quick, silly, breaks things by accident, learns a tiny lesson.

## ANIMAL SIDEKICK — "MOMO"

**Type:** Small fluffy cream chick/owl hybrid chick "Peep" (original).
**Role:** comic-relief observer; peeps/squeaks; follows the trio.
- **Color:** warm cream `#FFF6E5`, tiny, round, waddles.
- **Personality:** curious, mimics others, sweet.

---

## Style Guardrails (anatomy safety)
- NEVER 5-fingered hands — use round mitts.
- NEVER photorealistic humans.
- NEVER extra limbs, extra fingers, inconsistent eye count.
- Oversized expressive eyes preferred; avoid uncanny/narrow eyes.
- No text, logos, watermarks anywhere on the character.
